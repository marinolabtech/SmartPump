Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the application SmartPump: Fuel Prices (also known as SmartPump), developed by Marino Strazzullo (MarinoLab) ("the Developer"), processes information when you use it.

1. Data Controller

Developer / Controller: Marino Strazzullo (MarinoLab)
Contact Email: marino.strazzullo@gmail.com

2. Independence of the App

SmartPump is an independent app. It is NOT a government app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or authorised by any government body, ministry or public institution in Italy, France, Spain, Austria, Portugal or any other country. Fuel prices are taken from the public sources listed in section 4 and may be out of date or inaccurate.

3. Information Processed

SmartPump requires no account, contains no advertisements and uses no analytics or crash-reporting services. The Developer operates no server that receives your data. To function, the App processes the following:

  • Location (GPS): if you grant the location permission, the App uses your device location to show nearby fuel stations and calculate distances. Your location is processed on your device. The Developer never receives it. The App does not access location in the background. See section 4 for the countries where the coordinates of your search area are sent to the data provider.
  • Data saved on the device: your preferences (fuel type, search radius, country, theme, notifications), the last used location or map area, and downloaded station and price data. It stays on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its data in Android settings. The App is configured so that this data is not included in Android cloud backups.
  • Daily background update and price-drop notifications: if notifications are enabled, once a day (around 8:30, although Android may delay it) the App downloads updated prices in the background, using the last saved location or map area, and compares them on your device with the previous ones. If a price has dropped, it shows a local notification. You can disable notifications at any time in the App or in Android settings.
  • Voluntary donations: the App lets you support the Developer with an optional donation through Google Play Billing. Payment is handled entirely by Google. The Developer does not receive your card or payment details and only sees purchase information that Google makes available in the Play Console (such as amount and date). Donations do not unlock any feature.
  • Advertising: none. The App does not show ads and does not use the advertising identifier.

4. Data Sources, Third Parties and International Transfers

Station and price data are downloaded from the following public sources. All connections use HTTPS.

  • Italy: MIMIT (Ministry of Enterprises and Made in Italy), Osservaprezzi carburanti open data. National files are downloaded; your location is not sent.
  • Spain: Ministerio para la Transición Ecológica y el Reto Demográfico (MITECO), fuel price service. National file; your location is not sent.
  • Portugal: DGEG, Preços dos Combustíveis Online. National data; your location is not sent.
  • France: data.economie.gouv.fr, "Prix des carburants en France" (Ministry of Economy). The data is requested for an area around your location or the centre of the map you are viewing, so the coordinates of that point are sent to this service together with your IP address. If you are outside France, a default area is used.
  • Austria: E-Control, Spritpreisrechner. The data is requested for an area around your location or the centre of the map you are viewing, so the coordinates of that point are sent to E-Control together with your IP address. If you are outside Austria, a default area is used.

Other third parties involved in the operation of the App:

  • OpenStreetMap (map tiles): the map is based on data © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL, openstreetmap.org/copyright). To draw the map, your device downloads map tiles from OpenStreetMap servers, which receive your IP address and the area you are viewing. See the OpenStreetMap Foundation Privacy Policy.
  • Google (Google Play, in-app updates and Google Play Billing): for distribution, update checks and donation processing (Google Privacy Policy).

These services are run by independent organisations with their own privacy policies. Some of them may process data outside your country, including outside the European Economic Area. The Developer does not sell your data or share it for commercial purposes.

5. Legal Basis

Where the GDPR applies, location is processed on the basis of your consent, given through the Android permission prompt, which you can withdraw at any time in Android settings. Other processing is necessary to provide the service you request (finding stations and prices) and, for notifications, is based on your consent through the notification permission.

6. Data Retention

The Developer does not store location data or preferences on its own servers. Data saved on the device remains until you uninstall the App or clear its data. Retention by the third parties above is governed by their own policies.

7. Your Rights

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you have the right to request access, rectification or deletion of your personal data, to restrict or object to processing, and to withdraw consent. Since the Developer does not hold your data on any server, most data can be deleted by clearing the App data on your device. You may also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in Italy, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, garanteprivacy.it). For any request, write to the email address in section 10.

8. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and the Developer does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

9. Security and Changes

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections and the Developer keeps no user database. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The date of the last update is shown at the top of this page.
