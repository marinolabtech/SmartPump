Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the application SmartPump: Fuel Prices (also known as SmartPump), developed by Marino Strazzullo (Marino Lab) ("the Developer"), collects, uses, and shares information when you use our service.

1. Data Controller

Developer / Controller: Marino Strazzullo (Marino Lab)
Contact Email: marino.strazzullo@gmail.com

2. Collection and Use of Information

The application is designed to help you find fuel stations and the best prices near you. SmartPump does not require account creation and contains no advertisements. To function properly, the application processes the following data:

  • Location Data (GPS): The App requests access to your device's geographical location to calculate the distance to fuel stations and show you the closest ones. Location is processed directly on your device and is not saved or stored by the Developer on any servers. For countries whose data comes from OpenStreetMap (see section 3), the App may send the coordinates of the search point (your current location or the center of the map area you are viewing) to the Overpass service servers solely to retrieve the list of nearby fuel stations.
  • Data Saved on the Device: The App locally stores your preferences (fuel type, search radius, country, notifications), the last used location and map area, and downloaded station data. This data remains on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its data from Android settings.
  • Voluntary Donations: The App allows you to support the Developer with an optional donation via Google Play Billing. Payment is processed entirely by Google: the Developer does not receive or store your card or payment method details, and can only view purchase information made available by Google in the Play Console (such as transaction amount and date). Donations do not unlock any app features.
  • Advertising: The App does not contain advertisements and does not use the device's advertising identifier.

3. Data Sources and Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal or location data to external parties for commercial purposes. Station and pricing data originate from:

  • Official Public Databases: for Italy, data from MIMIT (Ministry of Enterprises and Made in Italy); for other countries where available, official public databases of the respective countries. This data is downloaded without sending your location.
  • OpenStreetMap: for countries without official open pricing data, station locations are retrieved from OpenStreetMap via the Overpass API service. Data is © OpenStreetMap contributors, made available under the ODbL license (openstreetmap.org/copyright). For these countries, displayed prices are indicative values rather than real-time individual station prices.

Third parties involved in the operation of the App include:

  • Overpass API Server Operators (e.g., overpass-api.de, overpass.kumi.systems, overpass.private.coffee, overpass.openstreetmap.fr): they receive the search coordinates and, as with any internet request, your device's IP address. These services are managed by independent entities separate from the Developer, with their own privacy policies.
  • Google (Google Play and Google Play Billing): for App distribution and donation processing (Google Privacy Policy).

4. Data Retention

The Developer does not store location data or user preferences on its own servers. Data saved on the device remains available until you uninstall the App or clear its data.

5. User Rights

If you reside in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you have the right to request access, rectification, or deletion of your personal data, and to object to its processing, within the limits provided by law. Since the Developer does not store user data on its servers, most data can be deleted directly by clearing the App data on your device. For any requests, you can write to the email address provided in the Contact section.

6. Data Security

We adopt reasonable security measures to protect the information processed by the application. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.
